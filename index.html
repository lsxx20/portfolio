What should every NBA contract pay?

An XGBoost model prices 898 player-seasons as a share of the salary cap — R² = 0.799 on a strict time split — and SHAP splits each valuation into basketball skill and market context. The dashed line is fair pay: dots above it earn less than the model sees, dots below earn more.

Method. Target is share of salary cap, never dollars — dollars are recovered through each season’s cap. Group B (market context) covers Bird rights, team payroll level, cap space, and team identity; everything else — production, efficiency, age, health, playoff elevation — is Group A, basketball skill. Skill and market contributions sum exactly to the model’s valuation. Trained on 2017–2022, tested on 2023–2024.
