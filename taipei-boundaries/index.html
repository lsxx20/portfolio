About this map

All layers come from official data. Layers built from villages (里) or lin (鄰) share the same edges. Hatched areas are land the official data does not assign to any unit.

Shapes are simplified for the web. Overlap percentages use the full-resolution data.

English names are official where one exists. Labels like “Polling station 0917” are descriptions.

Population is household registration by village, September 2026, shown only for layers made of whole villages.

Squares mark district offices, police, health centers, schools and fire stations. They sit at the official street address, placed with the Civil Affairs Department’s house-number coordinates; fire stations use the Fire Department’s own coordinates.

Base maps: Simple draws roads wider than 8 m (Public Works Department), river zones (urban-plan zoning map) and MRT stations (Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation). The street map and aerial photo (2025) are loaded live from the Taipei City Urban Development Department.
